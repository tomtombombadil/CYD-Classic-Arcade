Get the log

    
On the board, open Settings → Send Log (the gear at the top right of the game list) and point your phone's camera at the QR code. This page opens with the log.

    
Or the whole log on a computer: open Settings → USB Transfer on the board, then Download Log on the Load ROMs page (Chrome or Edge), and email the file it saves.
